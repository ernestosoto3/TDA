# Personas

The following personas represent realistic users within the Puerto Rican sports ecosystem. Each persona reflects real-world behaviors, motivations, and challenges observed in local sports fandom, athletics, and league administration.

The MVP needs and user stories describe the product capabilities required for Version 1. Long-term goals describe potential Post-MVP capabilities and are not acceptance criteria for the current MVP.

---

## Persona 1: Alfredo Guzman, the Casual Fan & Follower

**Background:**  
Alfredo is a 20-year-old Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Puerto Rico at Mayagüez. He is rarely seen without his favorite BSN team cap, a pair of worn-out sneakers, and a water bottle covered with stickers. He is constantly active in three different WhatsApp group chats arguing about basketball trades and game plays. He loves *empanadillas de camarones* on weekends, follows BSN and Liga de Béisbol Superior Doble A, and never misses a high-stakes rivalry game. However, he gets constantly frustrated having to jump between obscure Facebook pages, Instagram stories, and outdated league websites just to figure out what time a game starts.

### MVP Needs

* Quickly find verified game schedules, game statuses, venue information, and final scores.
* Receive reliable notifications about upcoming games, final results, postponements, cancellations, and schedule changes.
* Discover news, publications, teams, leagues, athletes, games, and communities in one centralized application.
* Add sports, leagues, teams, and athletes to Favorites to receive more relevant content.

### Long-Term Goals — Post-MVP

* Follow live scores and in-progress game events.
* Access advanced standings and detailed player-performance statistics.
* Find verified streaming links and richer game-media coverage.

### Frustrations

* Finding conflicting game times posted across different social media accounts.
* Missing games because of last-minute venue changes or unannounced schedule shifts.
* Fragmented sports coverage across league websites and social-media pages.
* Difficulty determining which information is current and verified.

### MVP User Stories and Feature Links

#### User Story 1 — Find Game Information

**As a casual sports fan,** I want to find upcoming games and verified final results so that I do not have to search through multiple league websites and social-media pages.

**Feature or use-case link:**  
Inicio, Buscar, game cards, and Detalles del juego.

#### User Story 2 — Receive Schedule Updates

**As a casual sports fan,** I want to receive notifications when a game is approaching or its schedule changes so that I can plan to attend or follow it.

**Feature or use-case link:**  
Notificaciones, Recordatorio de juego, Cambio de horario, and game-status updates.

#### User Story 3 — Personalize Sports Content

**As a casual sports fan,** I want to add my preferred teams, leagues, sports, and athletes to Favorites so that I can quickly access content related to them.

**Feature or use-case link:**  
Favoritos, Añadir a Favoritos, and the Inicio feed selector.

**Why this persona matters:**  
Alfredo highlights the MVP need for centralized and verified sports information, schedule-change notifications, content discovery, and Favorites-based personalization. Live scoring, advanced statistics, and streaming integration remain long-term opportunities.

## Persona 2: Valeria "Val" Colón, the Professional Athlete & Rising Talent

**Background:**  
Valeria is a 24-year-old professional volleyball player currently competing in Puerto Rico’s Liga de Voleibol Superior Femenino. Her life revolves around practices, physical training, and traveling to municipalities for games, with the long-term goal of securing a professional contract in Europe or being scouted by international teams.

She feels that information about Puerto Rican athletes is fragmented across team pages, league websites, social-media accounts, and isolated video posts. Although a complete scouting and recruitment platform is outside the MVP, Valeria still benefits from having verified sports information, team rosters, basic athlete statistics, news, and final results available in one searchable location.

### MVP Needs

* Appear in accurate and verified team rosters and basic athlete information.
* Allow users to find athletes, teams, leagues, games, and related news through Search.
* Provide centralized access to verified final results and basic game or athlete statistics.
* Connect athlete and team information to related news and publications.

### Long-Term Goals — Post-MVP

* Create and manage a polished professional athlete profile.
* Upload or organize game highlights and career footage.
* Display advanced, validated career statistics and performance metrics.
* Connect athletes with scouts, recruiters, agents, and professional opportunities.
* Support a dedicated scouting and recruitment pipeline.

### Frustrations

* Athlete information is fragmented across league websites and social-media accounts.
* Game footage is difficult to organize into a consistent professional portfolio.
* Basic career and performance information may be outdated or difficult to verify.
* Scouts outside Puerto Rico have limited access to organized information about local athletes.

### MVP User Stories and Feature Links

#### User Story 1 — Find Verified Athlete Information

**As an athlete,** I want my team affiliation, roster information, and available basic statistics to appear accurately so that fans and other users can find reliable information about me.

**Feature or use-case link:**  
Team Page, athlete search results, rosters, and basic athlete statistics.

#### User Story 2 — Discover Related Coverage

**As an athlete,** I want users to find news and publications connected to my team or sport so that my participation in local sports is easier to discover.

**Feature or use-case link:**  
Buscar, Noticias, Publicaciones relacionadas, and team-related content.

#### User Story 3 — Access Verified Results

**As an athlete,** I want completed games and final results to be published accurately so that users can review verified information about my team’s performance.

**Feature or use-case link:**  
Inicio, Detalles del juego, Marcador final, and Juegos anteriores.

**Why this persona matters:**  
Valeria highlights the MVP need for accurate and discoverable athlete, roster, team, and results information. Professional athlete profiles, video highlights, advanced career statistics, and scouting tools are explicitly reserved for Post-MVP development.

## Persona 3: Emeterio Santiago, the Local League Administrator

**Background:**  
Emeterio is a 52-year-old physical education teacher and youth league coordinator in Caguas. He is punctuality personified, always arriving 15 minutes early with a metal clipboard, a whistle around his neck, and a thermos of strong black coffee. He manages an eight-team adult basketball league and coordinates referees, court rentals, schedules, and official results.

Currently, he runs most of the operation using Excel spreadsheets, paper scorecards, and endless WhatsApp messages. Rain delays and court maintenance frequently disrupt his calendar, forcing him to manually update and message dozens of players and team captains individually.

### MVP Needs

* Create and update verified game schedules, dates, times, venues, and game statuses.
* Publish final scores and available basic game information.
* Update games as Programado, Pospuesto, Cancelado, or Final.
* Publish official news, schedule changes, and league announcements.
* Ensure that users can find updated league, team, and game information in the application.

### Long-Term Goals — Post-MVP

* Automatically calculate league standings from published results.
* Enter and manage detailed or advanced box scores.
* Coordinate referees, court assignments, and operational resources.
* Send bulk messages directly to players, coaches, and team representatives.
* Generate advanced league and athlete performance reports.

### Frustrations

* Manually entering results and maintaining information across spreadsheets and paper records.
* Answering repetitive messages about game times, venues, and schedule changes.
* Handling postponements and venue shifts caused by bad weather or court conflicts.
* Publishing the same update separately across several communication channels.

### MVP User Stories and Feature Links

#### User Story 1 — Publish and Update Schedules

**As a league administrator,** I want to create and update game dates, times, venues, and statuses so that users can access current and verified schedule information.

**Feature or use-case link:**  
Panel administrativo, Calendario de juegos, and the Programado, Pospuesto, Cancelado, and Final statuses.

#### User Story 2 — Publish Final Results

**As a league administrator,** I want to publish verified final scores after games end so that followers can see accurate results in the application.

**Feature or use-case link:**  
Panel administrativo, Marcador final, Inicio, and Detalles del juego.

#### User Story 3 — Communicate Schedule Changes

**As a league administrator,** I want to publish postponements, cancellations, venue updates, and official announcements so that affected users receive consistent information.

**Feature or use-case link:**  
Cambio de horario notifications, Anuncio oficial, Noticias, and game-status management.

**Why this persona matters:**  
Emeterio highlights the MVP need for administrative tools that support verified schedules, game statuses, final scores, and official updates. Automated standings, advanced box scores, referee coordination, and complete league-management automation remain Post-MVP goals.
